Buyers discount what they cannot verify. A complete file improves pricing and shortens the time to closing.

### Loan

- Note, endorsements, mortgage, and assignments
- Guaranties, modifications, and forbearance agreements
- Payment history and outstanding advances
- Title policy and security filings on personal property

### Franchise and management

- Franchise agreement and comfort letter
- Default and termination notices from the brand
- Improvement plan and latest quality inspection
- Management agreement

### Operations

- Three years of operating statements, plus year to date
- Monthly performance reports against the competitive set
- Occupancy and sales tax filings
- Liquor license and operating permits
- Group bookings and advance deposits

### Property and proceedings

- Latest appraisal, condition report, and environmental report
- Ground lease, if any
- Receiver's order and reports
- Pending litigation and bankruptcy filings
