A property improvement plan is the brand's list of required renovations. It is commonly triggered by a change of ownership, a renewal, or failed quality inspections.

An unfunded plan reduces the value of the collateral in three ways:

- the cost of the work itself;
- the revenue lost while rooms are out of service;
- the time the buyer carries the hotel until the work is finished.

If the work is not done, the brand can terminate. The hotel then loses the reservation system and loyalty program that supply much of its demand.

We value the hotel twice: once with the plan completed and the flag retained, and once as an independent or reflagged property. The lower-risk figure sets our basis.
