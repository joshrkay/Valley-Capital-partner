**What happened.** Two or three sentences, sourced and dated, with the measure and the publisher in the same sentence. Then the point: what a reader should take from it, plainly enough that stopping here would still leave them with the answer.

## Why it matters to a holder of a hotel loan

Start from the reader's situation, not the news. What decision does this touch, and what changes about it?

## How it works

The mechanics, in order, in the reader's terms. Restate what the site's Library and glossary already say, connect the parts, and apply them to this news.

## What is often confused

A distinction readers get wrong: what is mistaken for what, and why the difference matters.

**Illustration (invented numbers):** A small hypothetical with round, made-up figures that shows the mechanism at work.
