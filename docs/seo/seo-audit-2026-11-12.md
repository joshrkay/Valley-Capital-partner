# SEO audit: Valley Capital Partners site

Date: November 12, 2026. Scope: full site audit of the built site (60 pages, 59 indexable, `dist/` from the current branch), plus a keyword and competitor read from web search results.

## What this audit could and could not measure

- **Measured directly:** every title, description, heading, canonical, schema block, link, and sitemap entry on all 60 built pages. These are facts, not estimates.
- **Not measured:** search volume, keyword difficulty, backlinks, rankings, traffic, and Core Web Vitals. The Ahrefs connector failed to connect in this environment (proxy error), and the site is set to `noindex` and has not launched, so there is no Search Console data. Volume and difficulty below are **relative estimates from what ranks in search results**, not tool data. Treat the keyword table as a ranked shortlist to confirm in a keyword tool before committing writing time.
- **Competitors:** peer sites are blocked from this environment, so no competitor page was crawled. The comparison rests on who appears in search results and what those results show.

## Executive summary

The technical foundation is strong. Every page has a unique title and description, exactly one H1, a correct canonical, and a place in an accurate sitemap; there are no broken internal links and no orphan pages. The biggest strength is the content itself: the eight published posts are 723 to 756 words each and target the exact questions lenders and counsel search for.

Nothing can rank yet, by design. `robots.txt` disallows everything and `vercel.json` sends `X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow`, both marked as pre-launch. The three priorities with the most impact:

1. **Make the launch switch safe.** Flip robots and the header together, confirm the production domain (every canonical and sitemap entry uses `valleycapitalpartner.com` until `SITE_URL` is set), and submit the sitemap.
2. **Fix titles and descriptions.** 26 of 60 titles run over 60 characters (17 over 70), 19 descriptions run over 160, and 17 are too short to be useful. The homepage title has no keyword.
3. **Turn thin pages on high-value topics into real ones.** The five library notes (comfort letter, improvement plan, receivership, extend-sell-foreclose, checklist) are 165 to 222 words, yet they cover the topics law firms and trade press compete hardest on. The 71 glossary terms share one page, so none can rank for its own "what is" query.

Overall assessment: **strong foundation, needs work on metadata and thin pages before launch.** Authority is the long-term gap: the head terms are held by law firms, trade press, and brokers, so the realistic path is long-tail, buyer-side explainers plus earned links.

The useful finding is not who is stronger: it is that incumbents rank with PDFs and short announcements, so the bar on long-tail, plain-language, buyer-side questions is not high. Authority is the gap that takes time.
