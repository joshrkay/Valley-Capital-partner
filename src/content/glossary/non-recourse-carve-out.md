---
term: "Non-recourse carve-out (bad boy guaranty)"
---

A list of acts that remove the protection of a non-recourse loan, usually set out in a separate guaranty signed by the owner's principals. Typical entries are fraud, misusing property income, an unapproved transfer, and a voluntary bankruptcy filing. Depending on the wording, the guarantor becomes liable for the lender's losses from the act, or for the whole debt.
