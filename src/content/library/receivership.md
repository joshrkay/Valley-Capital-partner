A closed hotel loses its staff, its bookings, and often its flag. Most of the value in a receivership depends on continuous operation. Six points decide whether a hotel stays open.

- **The order of appointment.** It should give the receiver authority to operate, retain a manager, and control the accounts.
- **The franchise.** The brand has to agree to the receiver operating under the flag, often on a short-term basis.
- **The liquor licence.** It is usually in the owner's name, and interim arrangements differ by state.
- **Taxes.** Occupancy and sales taxes are collected on behalf of the government and must be kept current.
- **Payroll and insurance.** Both must continue without a gap.
- **Funding.** Shortfalls are covered by lender advances or by borrowing approved by the court.
