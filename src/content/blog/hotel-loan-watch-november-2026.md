Hotel loan delinquency in commercial mortgage-backed securities eased in October, and hotel results kept rising year over year. The latest lodging special servicing readings found are from the summer.

## The numbers

- **Trepp, lodging delinquency, October 2026: 5.94%.** That is 24 basis points below September's 6.18% ([Connect CRE](https://www.connectcre.com/?p=504241)). Earlier readings were 5.22% in June ([CRE Daily](https://www.credaily.com/?p=219707)) and 5.84% in August ([CRE Daily](https://www.credaily.com/briefs/cmbs-delinquency-holds-at-7-85-as-office-risk-rises/)).
- **Trepp, all property types, October 2026: 7.87%,** down from 8.02% in September. The fall came mainly from a $975 million showroom loan returning to current. Retail rose 63 basis points ([CRE Daily](https://www.credaily.com/?p=224934)).
- **KBRA, 30-plus-day delinquency, rated private-label CMBS, all property types, October 2026: 7.7%,** down 2 basis points from September ([KBRA](https://www.kbra.com/publications/qYPWnQCj)). KBRA's universe and method differ from Trepp's, so the two are not comparable. KBRA's latest lodging figure found is 6.4% as of March ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260603616364/en/KBRA-Releases-Research-Late-Checkout-Lodging-Delinquencies-Vary-by-Price-Class-and-Chain)).
- **CREFC, lodging special servicing, June 2026: 8.89%,** up 44 basis points on large new transfers ([CREFC](https://www.crefc.org/cre/content/News/Items/Research_and_Data/2026/CREFCs_June_2026_Monthly_CMBS_Loan_Performance_Report.aspx)). No later lodging reading was found. Across all property types, Trepp put special servicing at 11.42% in August, the highest since February 2013 ([CRE Daily](https://www.credaily.com/?p=222831)).
- **CoStar weekly data, October 2026:** revenue per available room was up year over year in each of the three weeks found: 5.5% for October 11 to 17 ([Hotel Online](https://www.hotel-online.com/news/us-hotel-performance-gains-momentum-as-revpar-rises-55)), 12.6% for October 18 to 24 ([Lodging](https://lodgingmagazine.com/costar-u-s-hotel-industry-reports-positive-year-over-year-comparisons-oct-29/)), and 9.7% for October 25 to 31 ([Hotel Online](https://www.hotel-online.com/news/us-hotel-performance-strengthens-in-late-october-as-revpar-jumps-nearly-10)). A single week can swing on local events, so read these as direction, not a monthly total. HVS's October market pulse put occupancy about two points below 2019 levels, with the gains coming from rate ([Hospitality Net](https://www.hospitalitynet.org/report/4135017/hvs-us-market-pulse-october-2026)).

## What moved

Lodging delinquency has run 5.22%, 5.84%, 6.18%, and 5.94% in June, August, September, and October. One large loan curing or defaulting can move a rate built on balances, as the overall October reading shows.

A loan that is extended or modified can leave the delinquent count without having been repaid. A lower rate does not by itself show that the underlying loans have been resolved.

## What is due next

- **Trepp's November reading:** typically in the first week of the following month, so early December.
- **KBRA's November CMBS report:** KBRA publishes near month-end. Its October 2025 edition is dated October 31, 2025 ([Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251031928676/en/KBRA-Releases-Research-CMBS-Loan-Performance-Trends-October-2025)).
- **FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile, third quarter:** late November, inferred from the second-quarter release in late August. It covers loans banks hold on their own books, which the securitized rates above do not.

## What we don't know

These rates cover securitized loans only. They say nothing about hotel loans held by banks. We have not compared Trepp's and KBRA's definitions line by line, and we did not find lodging special servicing for July through October.
